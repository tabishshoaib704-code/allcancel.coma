# Brick Breaker

A browser brick-breaker game with **455 levels**. Open `index.html` in any browser to play — no install or build step.

Every level is harder than the one before it:

- the ball gets faster (300 → 820 px/s) and the paddle gets smaller (90 → 34 px)
- red bricks take more hits to break (up to 7, the number on each brick) and turn darker the tougher they are
- silver steel bricks appear from level 6 and can't be broken
- from level 12 the whole wall creeps down toward you, faster each level, and costs a life if it reaches the red line
- power-ups get rarer

The look is a classic arcade style: hazard-striped walls, a grey tiled floor, textured red bricks, silver steel bricks and a blue paddle. Before each launch an aiming line sweeps back and forth — fire when it points where you want the ball to go.

Your highest level is saved in the browser, so you can continue where you left off.

**Controls:** move with the mouse, touch, or ← → / A D · launch with click, tap, or Space · pause with P.

**Power-ups:** W wide paddle · M multi-ball · S slow ball · ♥ extra life
